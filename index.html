Building AI to win back our time and our curiosity.

After twenty years in the high-pressure world of international finance, I traded the daily treadmill for a life built around freedom. From our home in Saigon I now build quiet, autonomous systems that take over the heavy digital work. That gives me room to step away from the screen, spend real time with my wife, and share the good moments together while the days rush past.

This site collects what I learn along the way. A few tools run my own work: a Hostinger VPS, Replit, Bitvavo and Claude. The others are research summaries: what the product does, what it costs and where the catch is, read from the vendor’s own pages and from what other buyers report.

How we got here

Beyond the rat race

For years I lived on a corporate rhythm that demanded constant attention. Now I use my technical background to build systems that give that attention back to what matters. Automating the complex digital work buys me the time to slow down days that would otherwise rush past.

01 / Deliberate presence

My goal is simple: keep my digital systems working quietly for me, so I can be fully present for the people I love most — without the constant pull of a screen.

02 / Keep learning

I use the time I win back to study, to reflect, and to stay curious. The world offers endless room to grow, as long as you step out of the daily rush long enough to see it.

03 / Supporting growth

Every day in Vietnam is a chance to guide our children as they discover a new culture. That shared experience as a family is priceless, and it is the whole reason I put digital autonomy first.

04 / A sustainable approach

I have seen demanding corporate life from the inside and know how precious our time is. My mission now is to share how you build something sustainable, deliberate and genuinely free.
